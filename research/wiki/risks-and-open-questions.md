## Product risks
- **Adoption.** Groups already trust each other; the tool must save the organizer real work.
- **Wallet onboarding.** Most target users have Wave, not a Lightning wallet. A demo needs a hosted wallet or a mobile money bridge.
- **Missed payments.** Non-custodial design means the app cannot force payment; it can only remind and record.
- **Regulation.** BCEAO rules on payment services were not reviewed.
- **Reputation value.** Portable records only matter if a lender accepts them.
