# Senegal Financial Inclusion

## Global Findex 2021 (Search-summary)
- 56% of adults owned an account (financial institution or mobile money), up from about 15% in 2014.
- Across WAEMU, roughly 75% of account owners use mobile money.
- Senegal is above the WAEMU average (about 41%).

## Implication
Mobile money (Wave, Orange Money) is the default way to pay. Inside Senegal, Bitcoin does not beat Wave on cost or familiarity. The case for Lightning is cross-border, see [[diaspora-remittances]].
