# MaTontine

Fintech that digitizes tontines in Senegal and West Africa, via smartphone app or SMS.

## How it works (Search-summary)
- Custody through regulated partners such as the microfinance institution COFINA, under BCEAO rules.
- Contributions and payouts over mobile money (Orange Money and others).
- Free for members. Revenue from commissions paid by partners (lenders, insurers) who use its credit-scoring data.
- Builds credit scores from saving behaviour; the data stays in its own platform.

## Gaps for [[tontinesats]]
- Cross-border members likely need a separate remittance step first (not confirmed).
- Reputation data is not portable.
- Closed source.
