# Differentiators

| Difference | Versus | Defensibility |
|---|---|---|
| Non-custodial, peer-to-peer payouts | [[matontine]] and [[zepargn]] hold funds through regulated partners | High |
| Cross-border by default over Lightning | Mobile money is national; diaspora members need a remittance step | Medium (corridor fees are only about 3-5%, see [[diaspora-remittances]]) |
| Portable reputation on Nostr | Competitors keep credit data in their own database | High in theory; needs a lender who accepts it |
| Open source, self-hostable | All competitors are closed | Medium; matches [[dakar-bitcoin-hack]] rules |

## Corrections to earlier claims
- "Zero intermediaries" is too strong. A member in France still buys sats with euros, and the beneficiary may convert sats to CFA. Both steps have spreads.
- "No licence needed" is a legal question that was not checked. Do not put it in the pitch.
- Self-published reputation is fakeable. Each payment record must be co-signed by the person who received it. See [[technical-building-blocks]].
