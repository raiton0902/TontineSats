# The SUSU App

Rotating savings circle app (US / Caribbean), built around the susu/esusu/tanda tradition.

## Model (Search-summary, App Store listing)
- Everyone contributes the same amount on the same schedule; one member receives the pot each round until the circle completes.
- Fixed, visible rotation from day one, shared record of every contribution, reminders, organizer confirmation.

## UI worth borrowing (Verified, screenshots)
- Home `UP NEXT` card: `Pay $200.00`, `Round 3 · due Wed, Sep 30 · 3 days left`, one primary `Contribute now` button.
- Segmented round bar with `4 paid · 2 to go`, one segment per member.
- Payout order list: `R1`…`R4` badges, `Received` green / `This round` amber / `Waiting` grey pills.
- Payout receipt: status card, four-step timeline with timestamps, `BREAKDOWN` and `DETAILS` blocks.
- Contribute sheet: amount hero, reassurance pill, `Rounds to cover` stepper, sticky pay button.
