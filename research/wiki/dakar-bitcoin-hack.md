# Dakar Bitcoin Hack

Online Devpost hackathon hosted by Btrust. Goal: tools built on Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr and AI that improve life in Senegal and Africa.

## Key facts (Search-summary)
- Deadline: November 20, 2026, 3:00 PM EST.
- Prizes: $900 in BTC total. 1st $500, 2nd $250, 3rd $150.
- Eligibility: reported as **students only**, no companies. Verify on the Devpost page.
- Must submit: presentation (text or video), a prototype/MVP/PoC, and at least a Bitcoin/Lightning wallet integration.
- Projects are expected to be open source and built in public.
