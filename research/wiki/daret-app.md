# Daret

Savings-circle app for Morocco (MAD), using the daret/tontine model.

## Model (Search-summary, App Store listing)
- Create a private circle with name, duration, monthly contribution, and start date.
- Invite with a code, set the payout order yourself.
- Organizer confirms contributions; the “circle gauge” fills as members pay.

## UI worth borrowing (Verified, screenshots)
- Dark green on cream, large friendly headings; a palette direction worth studying for “trust without bank blue”.
- Circle list rows with the amount right-aligned (`500 MAD`, `2400 MAD`).
- Small `Edit profile` pill and a quiet back chevron.

## What not to borrow
Full-bleed cover photos in the profile header. Weight and contrast risk on 3G.
