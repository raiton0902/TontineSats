# Diaspora Remittances

## Costs (Search-summary)
- Global average remittance cost: about 6.3%.
- Sub-Saharan Africa: the most expensive region, above 8%.
- France to Senegal corridor: roughly 3-5%, lower because of competition. Check exact figures at remittanceprices.worldbank.org.

## Diaspora tontines (Search-summary)
- Senegalese migrants, especially women, run tontines in France, sometimes with contributions of hundreds of euros per cycle (UvA, OpenEdition).
- Digital rails let diaspora members fund cycles back home.

## Unknown
How many Dakar tontines include members abroad. This number decides how strong the cross-border pitch is. See [[risks-and-open-questions]].
