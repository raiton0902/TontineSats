# Zepargn

Consumer savings app in the WAEMU zone (mainly Benin), by Digital Elevate SAS.

## Features (Search-summary)
- Group tontines, personal goals, locked deposits (Z-lock), microcredit (Z-flex).
- Mobile money gateways: MTN MoMo, Celtis, Kkiapay.
- Funds managed with AVM, a licensed decentralized financial institution (SFD) regulated by BCEAO.
- Free app; some transaction fees; 5% capital penalty plus lost interest on early Z-lock withdrawal.

## Relevance
Mainly Benin, so a weaker direct competitor in Senegal.
