# SÒL - Savings Circles

Savings-circle app for US and Caribbean diaspora communities (NAV COLLECTIVE). Closest philosophical match to [[tontinesats]] found so far.

## Model (Search-summary, App Store listing)
- Non-custodial: members pay each other directly by Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or cash. SÒL never holds or moves the money.
- The app keeps the record: members submit payment proof, the organizer confirms, the rotation stays visible.
- Members see what is due, their payout turn, and reminders; organizers see a per-round ledger.

## UI worth borrowing (Verified, screenshots)
- `Your next moves` card: exactly one action on the home screen.
- Member due card: `No contribution recorded` + due date + one `Record` button.
- Circle header: `Round 3 of 8`, `5 of 8 records confirmed` with a ring graphic.
- Trust Passport: ring gauge plus three stat columns. Do not copy the `92/100` composite score, which contradicts portable proof.

## Gap versus [[tontinesats]]
No Lightning, no `npub` identity, no cross-border rail. Proof is a photo and organizer review, not a payment preimage. See [[differentiators]].
