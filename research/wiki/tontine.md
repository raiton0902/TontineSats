# Tontine

A rotating savings and credit association (ROSCA). Members pay a fixed amount each period; one member takes the whole pot each round until everyone has received it once. Wolof names: *natt*, *tegg*.

## Why it persists (Search-summary)
- Fills the gap left by formal banking.
- Works as a zero-interest loan for whoever receives the pot early.
- Default rates stay very low because of social collateral, peer pressure and reputation, without legal enforcement (RePEc, Cairn).
- Participants are mostly economically active: about 90% wage earners and/or 64% in the informal sector, per one study (Emerald). Check against the original paper before citing.
- Women are heavy users, for trade financing and social security.

## Design lesson
Trust already exists inside the group. Software must not replace it; it should remove friction around it. See [[tontinesats]] and [[risks-and-open-questions]].
