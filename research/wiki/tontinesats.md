# TontineSats

## One-line pitch
A tontine you can join from anywhere, that never holds your money, and where your payment record belongs to you.

## Core design decisions
1. **Non-custodial.** Each round, members pay that round's beneficiary directly. The app tracks, reminds and verifies.
2. **CFA-denominated.** Members see "10,000 CFA". Lightning is the rail underneath; the beneficiary receives funds within minutes, so price exposure is short.
3. **Organizer-first.** The treasurer adopts the tool and brings the group. Members do not need to understand Bitcoin.
4. **Not an investment product.** Framed as a record-keeping and payment tool for an existing group.

## Primary personas (Assumption)
- Organizer: a woman market trader in Dakar who runs a circle of 5-15 people.
- Diaspora member: a Senegalese worker in France paying into a home circle.
