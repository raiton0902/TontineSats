# Wiki Schema

- `raw/` holds source notes (URLs and what was extracted). Never edit them after creation.
- `wiki/` holds one article per entity or concept. Link related pages with `[[page-name]]`.
- Every factual claim carries a status tag: **Verified**, **Search-summary**, or **Assumption**.
- `wiki/index.md` is the catalog; `wiki/log.md` records each research session.
